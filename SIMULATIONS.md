**9. Explanation**

**Status?** `ERP_PENDING`: Fineract has the repayment, the journal entry is owed. The partner got 202.

**Retried?** Yes. Recovery retries with backoff (2 s, 4 s, 8 s … capped at 300 s, at most 20 attempts), then hands the operation to a human as NEEDS_RECONCILIATION.

**How does the ERP catch up?** Recovery rebuilds the journal entry from the Fineract transaction stored with the operation and posts it; nothing is recomputed.

**How is double posting prevented?** Fineract is never called again, because its step is recorded as done. The ERP is unique on external_reference and compares payloads, so a repeated post returns the entry it already holds.
